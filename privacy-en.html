Privacy Policy

App Banconote 0 € (Zero Euro Souvenir) · Last updated: 24 October 2026

1. Who is the data controller

The data controller is Alessandro Ruzza, an individual. For any request about your data, write to zeroeurosouvenir@icloud.com.

2. What data we process

  • Account. Your email address, which you use to sign in with a link sent by email (there is no password). Optionally you can provide your name, city, country, year of birth and how you found the app; these are used for your profile and for aggregated statistics. We also store your language and notification choice.
  • Content you add. Places, banknote issues, confirmations and reports, your collection and the photos you upload. Published places and photos are visible to all users, but they do not show your name.
  • Location. Only if you grant permission and only while the app is in use. It is used to center the map near you and to indicate where a new place is. Your location is not stored or used to track your movements: if you add a place we store the coordinates of the place, not yours.
  • Photos. If you grant permission, the app accesses the camera or photo library only when you choose to add a photo. Photos of places and issues are reviewed by moderators before publication. Please do not upload photos showing recognizable people.
  • Technical data. IP address and device information processed by the third-party services listed below, as needed for them to work.

We do not use behavioral analytics tools or tracking across other companies' apps and websites.

3. Advertising

The app shows rewarded video ads through Google AdMob, in exchange for some features. Ads are non-personalized. For users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom we show Google's consent form on first launch; if you do not accept, no ads are shown and the features that require them stay locked. You can change your choice at any time from Profile → Privacy and data → Manage ad consent. Google may process device identifiers, IP address and ad interactions under its own policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. Third-party services

  • Supabase — database, sign-in and file storage. Data is hosted in Ireland (European Union).
  • OpenFreeMap — provides the maps; it receives your IP address and the map areas you view.
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) — place search; it receives the text you search for and your IP address.
  • Google AdMob — ads, as described above.
  • Apple — app distribution through the App Store.

5. Why we process data (legal bases)

  • To provide the service you request, such as sign-in, your collection and adding places (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • On the basis of your consent, for location, photos and advertising (Art. 6(1)(a)).
  • For our legitimate interest in keeping the platform secure, moderating content and preventing abuse (Art. 6(1)(f)).

6. How long we keep data

We keep account data until you delete your account. Places and photos already published remain available anonymously after the account is deleted, as they are a contribution to the community. Photos rejected by moderators are deleted.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account from Profile → Privacy and data → Delete my account. Deletion is immediate and removes your account, profile, collection, reports and private or pending photos. Your name does not remain associated with published content.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict processing, to receive your data in a readable format (portability) and to object to processing; you can withdraw any consent at any time. Write to zeroeurosouvenir@icloud.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority (garanteprivacy.it) or your local authority.

9. Transfers outside the European Union

Some providers, such as Google, may process data outside the European Economic Area. They do so with the safeguards provided by the GDPR, such as standard contractual clauses or the Data Privacy Framework.

10. Children

The app is not directed to children under 14 and we do not knowingly collect data from children of that age. If you believe a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how we process data, we will update this page and the date above. For important changes we will notify you in the app.
