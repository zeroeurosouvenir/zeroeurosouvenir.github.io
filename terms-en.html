0 Euro Souvenir app · Last updated: 30 October 2026

1. What the app does

The app helps collectors find where to buy 0 euro souvenir banknotes and keep track of their collection. Information about places is added by the community and may be incomplete or out of date: always check before you travel.

0 euro banknotes are collectible souvenirs and are not legal tender. The app is not affiliated with the European Central Bank or the banknote manufacturers.

2. Account and nickname

To use the social features you need a nickname, which must not be offensive or impersonate others. We may reset it (you then choose another) or remove you from the leaderboard. The app is not directed to anyone under 14.

3. Adding banknotes to your collection

  • A banknote is added to your collection only by scanning it (front and back) with the app's scanner.
  • You can add several copies of the same banknote: each copy is identified by its serial number and counts only once for points.
  • Anyone who circumvents or abuses the system may be excluded and lose their points.
